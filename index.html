The long way across Asia

Four cities, four networks, and a route traced through metro platforms, airport rails, night buses, station halls, and waterfronts.

Stop 01 · UAE

Dubai 🇦🇪

DXB / 25.2048° N

From the field notebook

“Scale changes what feels close. Every elevated road, station, and tower turns distance into a carefully drawn line.”

Dubai moves vertically. The Metro's driverless trains make the city's ambitions legible between glass towers, desert light, and airport connections.

Stop 02 · Taiwan

Taipei 🇹🇼

TPE / 25.0330° N

From the field notebook

“The mountain weather arrives in the station reflections: green signs, rain-washed tiles, and a train arriving on time.”

Taipei is intimate and layered. Metro concourses carry people under lively neighbourhoods, while buses and airport rail connect dense streets to the wider island.

  • Taoyuan Airport MRT
  • Metro Taipei
  • City buses
  • Railway stations

Stop 03 · Thailand

Bangkok 🇹🇭

BKK / 13.7563° N

From the field notebook

“Above the traffic, the elevated rail moves in a second city—interchanging heat, colour, river crossings, and sound.”

Bangkok rewards looking both up and across. Skytrain viaducts, river boats, colourful terminals, and buses reveal a city made of overlapping routes.

  • BTS Skytrain
  • Airport Rail Link
  • MRT
  • River boats & buses
  1. 06:45Airport Rail Link

Stop 04 · Singapore

Singapore 🇸🇬

SIN / 1.3521° N

From the field notebook

“The network feels seamless because every pause is considered—from the platform edge to the tree line above it.”

Singapore brings transit, landscape, and architecture into one precise visual system. MRT interchanges and airport links make long journeys feel effortless.

  • Changi Airport skytrain
  • Bus interchanges
